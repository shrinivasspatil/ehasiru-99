About This Service

India generates over 3 million metric tonnes of e-waste annually, yet only a fraction is recycled through authorised channels. Improper disposal contaminates soil and water, creates legal liability, and violates the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2016. E-Hasiru operates as a fully CPCB-authorised e-waste recycler, providing enterprises with a compliant, documented, and environmentally responsible recycling pathway for all categories of electronic waste.

What You Get

  • CPCB-authorised recycling under E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2016
  • Full dismantling and material recovery at our Bangalore facility
  • Hazardous material handling compliant with CPCB norms
  • Form 6 documentation for all recycled material
  • CO2 savings and material recovery report
  • Pan-India pickup — 245+ cities covered
  • Available as a managed annual programme for large enterprises
