About This Service

Not every end-of-life asset needs to be recycled. Assets with remaining functional value can be refurbished, tested, and remarketed — reducing e-waste, recovering residual value, and supporting the circular economy. E-Hasiru's refurbishment programme evaluates, repairs, upgrades, and certifies used IT assets, giving them a second productive life while keeping your disposal process fully compliant.

What You Get

  • Functional assessment and grading of all received assets
  • Hardware repair, component replacement, and OS re-imaging
  • Quality testing to ensure refurbished devices meet performance standards
  • Certified data destruction before any refurbishment begins
  • Documentation of refurbishment outcome per asset
  • Remarketing through responsible channels
  • Value recovery report — what was recovered vs recycled

Certifications & Compliance

  • Data destruction before refurbishment — always
  • CPCB Authorised for non-refurbishable components
  • R2v3 Certified responsible remarketing
  • Full chain of custody documentation
