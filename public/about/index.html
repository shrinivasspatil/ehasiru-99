Our Story

E-Hasiru is a Bengaluru-based, R2v3-certified e-waste recycling company established in 2014, delivering end-to-end IT asset disposal (ITAD) and secure data destruction across India.

We partner with IT teams, procurement heads, and compliance officers to ensure 100% secure, traceable, and regulation-compliant disposal of IT assets — while unlocking value through refurbishment and responsible recycling.

Operating across 245+ cities, we have processed over 12,000 MT of e-waste and served 124+ corporate clients — from IT companies and startups to manufacturing firms and educational institutions. Every disposal is documented, auditable, and fully compliant.

Mission

To provide innovative and sustainable e-waste recycling solutions that protect the environment, promote responsible disposal, and maximise resource recovery.

Vision

To be India's most trusted IT asset disposal and e-waste management partner — setting the standard for environmental responsibility, data security, and operational excellence.
